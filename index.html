Information

"Valorant Agents emerged following the First Light, a global event that unleashed a powerful energy source called Radianite. Agents derive their abilities from three distinct origins: being Radiants with innate elemental superpowers, utilizing advanced Radianite technology, or bonding with ancient, mystical artifacts that grant them supernatural control."

Facts

The world of VALORANT is split into two parallel realities, Alpha Earth and Omega Earth, following a cosmic event called the First Light.

  • Alpha Earth is trying to defend its resources.
  • Omega Earth is suffering an environmental collapse and must steal Radianite from Alpha Earth to survive.

This creates a tragic, interdimensional war for survival with no true villains, which perfectly explains why identical agents fight each other in the game.
